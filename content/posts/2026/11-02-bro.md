Even though models have gotten much better at explaining things, they sometimes still end up dumping a wall of text that is very painful to go through.

[Dillon Mulroy’s `/bro` skill](https://github.com/dmmulroy/skills/blob/main/bro/SKILL.md) cuts through the crap surprisingly well. The entire prompt is:

> Restate your last message. Stop using jargon and speak coherently. State it more simply and concisely, like one human talking to another.

Use it when an agent’s explanation gets too dense. You can install it as a skill or prompt template and invoke `/bro`, or just paste the prompt.
