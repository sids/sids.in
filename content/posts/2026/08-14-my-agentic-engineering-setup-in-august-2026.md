Noting my agentic coding setup, mostly as a way to share with others what I’m doing (and hopefully get inputs on what I can do better).

## Models & Subscriptions

My daily driver is GPT-5.6 Sol (high) accessed through the ChatGPT Pro 20x ($200/month) subscription.[^1]

I have an OpenCode Go subscription ($10/month) for accessing the open weights models. I mostly play around with them when a new model comes out, and occasionally for simpler tasks.

I presently don’t have a Claude subscription, mostly because I’m turned off by the entire holier-than-thou rhetoric coming from Anthropic; to say nothing about their seeming distaste for anything open (not open spending the harness, tallying against open weights, not supporting open standards like AGENTS.md or .agent/skills/).[^2]
## Coding Harness & App

It’s [Pi](https://pi.dev/) all the way for me. It’s super simple and highly customisable (with extensions that Pi can build itself) while still being extremely powerful (due to modelling the sessions as a /tree and the aforementioned extensions).

I do keep playing with [Amp Code](https://apmcode.com/) once in a while, but I see myself going back to Pi every time. Maybe they’ll finally get with their [Agents in Orbs](https://ampcode.com/news/agents-in-orbs)? We’ll see.

I use the [Codex desktop app](https://chatgpt.com/codex/) for some specific things like building Codes plugins (for work), building iOS/Mac apps (because of excellent plugins for the same), and for learning some new things (because of the in-built browser). It is a well-built app though a little slow for my liking (both the app _and_ the harness — every task takes more time in Codex compared to Pi).[^3]

I have tried OpenCode (desktop and tui) and T3Code, but they have not stuck.
## Terminal Setup

Pi is a terminal app (a web view is in the works). So I end up spending a lot of time in the terminal and that setup becomes important.

[Ghostty](https://ghostty.org/) has been my terminal of choice for a year and a half now. It’s solid and very Mac-native. I’m presently using the Nord theme and SauceCodePro Nerd font (I keep changing these once in a while for some freshness).

Within Ghostty, I use [herdr](https://herdr.dev/) as a terminal multiplexer to run all my agents. I was a long-term tmux user but I cooled on it a few years ago. I tried mining to Zellij but it doesn’t stick.
