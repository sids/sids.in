Chris Kiehl:

> Reading Hacker News makes me feel like I need to write this: it's still OK to enjoy programming.

> Yes, at work, where you are paid to produce value for a business, the economics of purely hand-written code have changed. But that's work. Work is not life. The loudest voices on the internet are the saddest and most infectious.

> It's OK to enjoy things. Doing so requires no economic justification.

I feel this. There was a time, when I was young, that I used to program purely for fun. For a few years, my only hobby was programming. Sad? Maybe (or maybe not).

But for the last year or so, I have felt that writing code by hand was somehow “wrong”. I’m going to change that.
